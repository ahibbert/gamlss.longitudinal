# Deferred multivariate benchmark

The exploratory Module 09 multivariate/copula-selection benchmark is not part of
the active JSS submission evidence set. It is not run by the reviewer `smoke`,
`paper`, or `full` profiles, does not support an active manuscript claim, and is
not included in the Phase 2 artifact allowlist.

The implementation is preserved under
`paper/R/09-simulation-multivariate-longitudinal/` for future development. Its
former artifact and claim identifiers (`p2_mvt_*` and the
`multivariate-benchmark` study) are intentionally deferred rather than treated
as submission evidence. Re-activating it requires a separately reviewed design,
versioned input schema, tractable reviewer runtime, manuscript scope decision,
and new artifact-contract entries.
