# JSS developmental review round 1

This directory records independent developmental reviews of the frozen
canonical manuscript build described in `review-bundle-manifest.md`.

The reports assess a working draft. They are evidence for prioritising the next
revision, not edits to the manuscript and not an editorial decision by JSS.

## Outcome

All eight reviewers recommend major revision or resubmission rather than
submission of the current draft. They consistently regard the software niche as
promising, but identify correctness, evidence-alignment, and release-state work
that must precede editorial polishing.

The corrected backlog contains 35 normalized actions: 14 P0 items that must close
before submission, 18 P1 major changes, and three P2 finishing tasks. P0 is a
closure priority, not execution order; `next-steps.md` gives the acyclic order.

## Start here

- `synthesis.md` - concise consensus, disagreements, and recommended paper thesis.
- `next-steps.md` - decision-complete six-phase implementation sequence.
- `issue-register.csv` - evidence, owner, effort, dependencies, and observable
  acceptance criteria for every normalized issue.
- `figure-table-audit.csv` - disposition of all 18 figures and 21 tables.
- `meta-review.md` - adversarial audit and post-correction confirmation that the
  bundle is safe to guide implementation.
- `review-bundle-manifest.md` - exact manuscript/package identity and build hashes.
