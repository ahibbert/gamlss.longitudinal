ACTG final-model shortlist review

Three ZISICHEL–Gumbel models fitted to the full ACTG data, with subject-clustered sandwich inference, publication coefficient tables, complete diagnostics, and fixed/smooth term plots.

Integrity check: all three reports completed 16/16 artifact stages; every coefficient has finite robust and model-based SEs, 95% CIs, and p-values. The visit-1 interaction plots omitted by the generic plotter were added separately.
Inference caution: all three models required the validated numerical-Hessian fallback and carry the advisory fitted_gradient_too_large warning. The covariance matrices are returned, but inferential results should be interpreted cautiously.
